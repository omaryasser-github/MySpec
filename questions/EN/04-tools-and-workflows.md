# Category 4: Tools & Workflows (Q26–Q32)

## Q26: Primary Software Stack
**Question:** What is your primary development stack, daily engineering tools, languages, and platforms that power your core workflow?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Main tools, frameworks, and programming platforms
  - Software subscriptions and developer environments
  - Integration workflows and toolchain dependencies

## Q27: Operating System & Devices
**Question:** What operating systems, hardware setups, and primary machines do you use, and do you have any device or resource constraints?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Primary OS (macOS, Windows, Linux distro)
  - Hardware specifications and development machines
  - Hardware, containerization, or virtualization constraints

## Q28: Project Management Approach
**Question:** What project management methodology (e.g., Kanban, Scrum, GTD) and issue-tracking tools do you rely on to plan and execute work?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Work management philosophy (Agile, Kanban, GTD, Shape Up)
  - Tools and ticketing systems (Jira, Linear, GitHub Projects)
  - Planning cadences and review rhythms

## Q29: Writing & Documentation Tools
**Question:** What tools, formats, and markup languages (e.g., Markdown, Notion, Obsidian) do you prefer for documentation and technical notes?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Preferred writing and note-taking platforms
  - Markup preferences (Markdown, AsciiDoc, Notion, Confluence)
  - Collaboration and knowledge-base sharing needs

## Q30: Communication Platforms & Rhythm
**Question:** What are your primary communication tools, and do you lean toward asynchronous written communication or real-time meetings?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Primary messaging platforms (Slack, Discord, Teams, Email)
  - Preference between async documentation vs. synchronous calls
  - Availability and interruption boundaries

## Q31: Automation Capabilities & CI/CD
**Question:** What is your comfort level with automation, scripting, and CI/CD pipelines, and what automation tooling do you actively use?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Comfort with shell scripting, GitHub Actions, Docker, Makefiles
  - Current automation footprint in daily tasks
  - Willingness to adopt new automation tools

## Q32: Data & Analytics Stack
**Question:** What databases, analytics platforms, caching layers, and observability tools do you regularly work with or monitor?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Primary databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, SQLite, etc.)
  - Caching and queuing systems (Redis, Kafka, RabbitMQ)
  - Logging, monitoring, and telemetry tools
