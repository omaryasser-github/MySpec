# Category 7: Personal Context (Q45–Q50)

## Q45: Location & Timezone
**Question:** What is your primary geographical location and timezone, and what regional or latency considerations affect your daily work?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Geographic region and city
  - Time zone offset (UTC / local)
  - Regional network, infrastructure, or localization needs

## Q46: Energy & Productivity Patterns
**Question:** During what hours of the day is your focus and cognitive energy at its peak, and when do your energy dips typically occur?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Peak performance and deep-work hours
  - Cognitive energy fluctuations across the day
  - Night owl vs. early bird cadence

## Q47: Learning Style & Retention
**Question:** What is your preferred learning modality—hands-on building, reading documentation/source code, or video walk-throughs—and what best solidifies your retention?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Hands-on experimentation vs. structured reading vs. video tutorials
  - Learning pace preference
  - Retention reinforcement techniques (building notes, toy projects, teaching)

## Q48: Health, Ergonomics & Accessibility Needs
**Question:** Do you have any ergonomic, sensory, or cognitive constraints (e.g., ADHD focus patterns, eye strain, RSI) that should inform how technical guidance is formatted?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Physical or ergonomic factors (RSI, posture, eye fatigue)
  - Cognitive or focus preferences (ADHD-friendly chunking, low distraction)
  - Visual accessibility needs (contrast, font density)

## Q49: Life Situation & Schedule Boundaries
**Question:** What current personal responsibilities or life stage factors (e.g., family care, studies, secondary commitments) set boundaries on your weekly availability?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Family or caregiving responsibilities
  - Competing academic or business commitments
  - Rigid scheduling constraints vs. flexible hours

## Q50: Recharge & Burnout Prevention
**Question:** What activities, offline habits, or practices best restore your energy and protect you from technical fatigue when away from code?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - What actively restores mental clarity
  - Offline hobbies and stress relief
  - Renewal practices and boundary protection
