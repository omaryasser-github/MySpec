# Category 2: Communication Style (Q11–Q18)

## Q11: Response Length Preference
**Question:** What is your preferred depth and detail level when an AI answers your technical questions, and when should it be concise versus comprehensive?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Preferred depth and detail level
  - When to be concise vs. comprehensive
  - Threshold for asking follow-up questions

## Q12: Format Preference
**Question:** What formatting structure do you prefer for explanations—bullet points, prose, step-by-step guides, or structured tables?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Bullet points vs. prose
  - Use of headers and visual hierarchy
  - Code-first vs. explanation-first preference

## Q13: Tone & Voice
**Question:** What tone and personality do you prefer in your technical interactions—formal, casual, strictly direct, or collaborative?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Formal vs. casual
  - Technical vs. conversational
  - Persona and partnership style

## Q14: Disclaimers & Caveats
**Question:** How should the AI handle uncertainty and risk—do you prefer direct, unhedged answers or explicit caveats and disclaimers?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Preference for hedging language
  - Risk tolerance vs. directness balance
  - How to state edge-case uncertainty

## Q15: Technical Terminology
**Question:** What is your comfort level with advanced jargon, and when should specialized domain terms be explained versus assumed?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Jargon comfort level
  - When to explain foundational concepts vs. assume domain knowledge
  - Industry-specific vocabulary preference

## Q16: Option Presentation
**Question:** When choosing between technical paths, do you prefer a single opinionated recommendation first, or a comparative trade-off analysis of multiple options?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Single best recommendation first
  - Ranked lists vs. pros/cons trade-off tables
  - Depth of comparative analysis

## Q17: Feedback & Critique Style
**Question:** How do you prefer receiving feedback or code reviews—unvarnished and direct, or supportive with context and positive reinforcement?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Need for affirmation before critique
  - Preference for direct vs. cushioned feedback
  - Handling of architectural disagreements

## Q18: Formatting & Aesthetics
**Question:** What are your visual presentation preferences regarding emoji usage, markdown emphasis, bolding, and code callouts?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Emoji usage (none, minimal, expressive)
  - Bold, italics, and highlighting density
  - Visual structure and code snippet styling
