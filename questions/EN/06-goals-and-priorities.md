# Category 6: Goals & Priorities (Q39–Q44)

## Q39: 90-Day Goal
**Question:** What is your single most critical objective or delivery milestone to accomplish over the next 90 days, and what are its success criteria?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Specific near-term technical or project objective
  - Measurable checkpoints and key dates
  - Concrete definition of completion

## Q40: 1-Year Strategic Vision
**Question:** What is your primary 1-year career vision or technical growth milestone, and what major outcome will signal its achievement?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Long-term technical or career direction
  - Major architectural or organizational outcomes
  - Targeted seniority, authority, or impact level

## Q41: Intentional Deprioritizations (Intentional "No's")
**Question:** What technical topics, tools, or projects are you deliberately deprioritizing or saying "no" to in order to protect your core focus?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Explicitly deprioritized languages or frameworks
  - Out-of-scope initiatives or distractions
  - What you have consciously chosen *not* to build or learn right now

## Q42: Non-Negotiable Boundaries
**Question:** What are your core non-negotiable principles, engineering ethics, or lifestyle boundaries that you will not compromise on?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Hard operational and ethical constraints
  - Code quality or testing minimum standards
  - Work-life balance and schedule boundaries

## Q43: Extra Time Allocation
**Question:** If you had 5 additional uninterrupted hours in your technical work week, what high-leverage activity would you invest them in?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Investment in learning vs. refactoring vs. prototyping
  - Unmet priorities revealed by hypothetical extra capacity
  - High-impact growth areas waiting for available time

## Q44: Definition of "Enough"
**Question:** What is your personal definition of "enough"—how do you recognize when a feature, architectural refactor, or work session has reached sufficient completeness?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Sufficiency threshold vs. perfectionism
  - Metric for "good enough for production"
  - Criteria for stopping further iteration or optimization
