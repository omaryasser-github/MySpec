# Category 1: Identity and Work (Q01–Q10)

## Q01: Current Role & Responsibilities
**Question:** What is your current role or job title, and what are your core day-to-day responsibilities and primary deliverables?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Job title and actual day-to-day work
  - Specific responsibilities and deliverables
  - Primary focus areas

## Q02: Industry & Market Context
**Question:** What industry vertical and market segment do you operate in, and what does your typical customer or client profile look like?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Industry vertical (e.g., Fintech, SaaS, E-commerce, HealthTech)
  - Customer or client profile
  - Market segment or niche

## Q03: Experience Level
**Question:** How many years have you been in your current field, and what has your career progression looked like?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Years of hands-on experience in current field
  - Career progression and seniority trajectory
  - Relevant technical background

## Q04: Team Structure
**Question:** What is your team size and structure, and what is your specific role and reporting relationship within the team?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Team size and makeup
  - Your role within the team (IC, lead, collaborator)
  - Reporting structure and stakeholder touchpoints

## Q05: Current Projects
**Question:** What primary project or key technical initiative are you currently working on, including its scope, milestones, and upcoming deadlines?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Primary project or initiative
  - Key milestones and delivery deadlines
  - Project scope and technical impact

## Q06: Work Schedule & Rhythm
**Question:** What does your typical daily work schedule look like, including your peak productivity hours, timezone, and work location?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Typical daily schedule and focus windows
  - Peak productivity hours
  - Time zone and work location (remote, hybrid, on-site)

## Q07: Budget & Resource Context
**Question:** What resource, budget, or financial constraints govern your work, and how are technical investments or subscriptions allocated?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Annual revenue or budget range
  - Resource allocation (cloud, tooling, hardware)
  - Financial or infrastructure constraints

## Q08: Previous Roles & Transferable Experience
**Question:** What previous roles or technical domains have you worked in, and what transferable skills or mental models did you bring with you?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Prior experience and roles
  - Transferable technical and operational skills
  - Career trajectory shifts

## Q09: Key Frustrations & Blockers
**Question:** What are your biggest day-to-day technical frustrations, bottlenecks, or blockers that slow down your momentum?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Current workflow pain points
  - Technical bottlenecks or blockers
  - System or process areas needing improvement

## Q10: 12-Month Success Definition
**Question:** What concrete outcomes, key metrics, or accomplishments will define a successful year for you 12 months from now?
- **Context & Guidance:**
  - Objective success metrics
  - Key personal or team objectives
  - Desired career and project outcomes
